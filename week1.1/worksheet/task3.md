## State two advantages of using version control when writing software. 
### the perspective of an individual programmer
    They can view their works either on university machines or their own machines.Also, they can backup their works prevent lost.
### the perspective of working in a team developing software.   
    Work on the same codebase with other people and it is live.
## State one disadvantage of Git
### your own perspective
    Increased unnecessary complexity and maintenance burden for programmer.
### the perspective of a new programmer who has just been introduced to it. 
    It's difficult to get started for new programmer because they should rember a lot of commands.
